01 / CAPABILITIES

EDIT SMARTER,
NOT LONGER

01

AI Transcription

WhisperX-powered transcription with word-level timestamps and forced alignment. Edit your video by editing text.

02

Silence & Filler Removal

Automatically detect and cut dead air, "um"s, "uh"s, and filler words. Tighten your edit in seconds.

03

Repetition Detection

Find repeated takes and stammered phrases automatically. Keep the best take, cut the rest.

04

Speaker Diarization

Identify who's speaking when. Color-coded speakers in your transcript for multi-person content.

05

Scene Detection

Automatically detect scene changes and visual transitions. Navigate your footage by scene, not by scrubbing.

06

Resolve Integration

Native DaVinci Resolve API integration. Changes apply directly to your timeline — no exporting, no round-tripping.

02 / WORKFLOW

THREE STEPS.
THAT'S IT.

1

Open FinlayCut

Launch the app alongside DaVinci Resolve. It connects automatically via the Resolve scripting API.

2

Analyze Your Timeline

Transcribe, detect silence, find filler words, identify speakers — all with one click per feature.

3

Apply Edits

Review the suggestions, tweak if needed, then apply. FinlayCut makes the cuts directly in your Resolve timeline.
